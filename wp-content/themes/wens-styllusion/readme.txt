== Description ==

WENS Styllusion is a modern, feature-rich eCommerce WordPress theme designed as a child theme of WENS Haelo. It combines a sleek, stylish design with powerful functionality, making it the ideal choice for fashion entrepreneurs and online store owners looking to launch their businesses quickly and efficiently. Whether you’re selling fashion, furniture, clothing, sports gear, or fitness products, WENS Styllusion provides a clean, user-friendly platform to showcase your inventory. Fully compatible with WooCommerce, it simplifies product listings, order management, and secure transactions, ensuring a smooth shopping experience for your customers. The theme includes pre-designed sections such as Home, Shop, Men, Women, Kids, and Brand, which contribute to an organized and visually appealing store layout. With an intuitive search bar and built-in “Add to Cart” functionality, browsing and purchasing products is seamless. Moreover, the fully customizable design allows you to modify fonts, colors, button styles, and section opacity without any coding knowledge, helping you create a store that perfectly aligns with your brand identity. Built for performance and user engagement, WENS Styllusion is fully responsive and SEO-friendly, ensuring that your store looks great and functions smoothly on all devices. It also integrates with the Jetpack plugin, providing essential tools for performance optimization, subscription management, and enhanced site functionality. Whether you’re running a cutting-edge fashion store or managing an online shop across various categories, WENS Styllusion is the perfect platform to showcase your products with style, offering all the essential tools to elevate your eCommerce business.


== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in WENS Styllusion in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on the 'Activate' button to use your new theme right away.
4. Navigate to Appearance -> Editor in your admin panel and customize to taste.
